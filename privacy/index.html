LiftMax Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

LiftMax is a workout tracking app. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as important, what it doesn't.

Your data

All information you enter — your name, age, height, weight, sex, fitness goals, workouts, exercises, routines, and history — is stored in a local database on your device. The app has no user accounts and no sign-up. We (the developer) have no way to see, access, or recover this data, and it is never included in the crash reports described below.

It stays on your device unless you switch on backup to Google Drive, which is described next. Nothing is uploaded until you ask for it.

Backing up to your Google Drive

LiftMax can copy your data to your own Google Drive, so that a lost or replaced phone doesn't mean starting over. This is off until you turn it on: nothing leaves your device until you connect a Google Account, and the app asks you to confirm what will be uploaded before it does.

If you switch it on, the copy contains the same information listed above — your profile, workouts, exercises, routines and history. It is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a private folder in your own Google Drive, on your own storage. That folder is a hidden application-data folder: it is not visible when you browse your Drive, no other app can read it, and LiftMax cannot see or ask to see anything else in your Drive. There is no LiftMax server involved, and no copy is kept anywhere else — the data goes from your phone to your Google Account and nowhere in between.

You can optionally turn on end-to-end encryption, which scrambles the backup on your phone before it is sent, using a key held only on that device. With it on, the file in your Drive cannot be read by Google, by us, or by anyone who reaches it — only by someone who has your key. The key is never uploaded, which also means that losing both your key and your phone makes the backup permanently unreadable. The app says so when you switch it on.

You are in control of the copy. You can disconnect your Google Account, delete the backup from your Drive from within the app at any time, or turn automatic backup off. Uninstalling LiftMax causes Google to delete the application-data folder along with the app. Your Google Account and Drive are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Crash and error reporting

LiftMax uses Sentry, a third-party error-monitoring service, to learn about crashes and bugs. When the app crashes or runs into an unexpected error, Sentry automatically receives technical diagnostic information: the error message and stack trace, characteristics of your device such as its model, manufacturer, operating system version and app version, and a short technical history of app events (such as screen navigation or network status changes) leading up to the error. This is used solely to identify and fix bugs. The app is configured so that your IP address is not recorded with these reports, and it does not attach your name or any other profile detail to them. See Sentry's privacy policy for how they handle this data. LiftMax does not use analytics, advertising, or any other tracking software.

How crash data is handled and how long it is kept

Crash reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and are processed and stored in Sentry's European data region, in Frankfurt, Germany. Access is limited to the developer of LiftMax. Sentry automatically deletes stored crash reports once its retention period elapses, after which we no longer hold them. If you would like a crash report associated with your device removed sooner, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Data sharing

Your training data is never shared with anyone. If you turn on backup it is sent to your own Google Drive, at your instruction and to storage you already own — it is not passed to us or to any third party for their own purposes, and there is no LiftMax server that could receive it. Crash diagnostics (described above) are shared only with Sentry, and only for the purpose of debugging. Nothing is ever sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

Data deletion

You can erase everything from inside the app, or delete all app data by removing the app from your device — uninstalling LiftMax permanently removes your local database along with it.

If you have backed up to Google Drive, the app has a "Delete Backup from Drive" action that removes the copy from your Google Account. Uninstalling also causes Google to delete the application-data folder the backup lives in. Deletion of crash diagnostics is covered in the section above.

Children's privacy

LiftMax does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Everything you enter stays on your device, and crash reports contain only technical diagnostic information, never anything you've entered into the app.

Changes to this policy

If LiftMax's data practices change further, this policy will be updated first and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
