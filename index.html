    Running the app's own formulas, not a mock-up of them. Two simplifications: each session here is summarised by one set where the app grades every set you log, and the per-lift personalisation — your rep-max curve, your reps-in-reserve bias — needs more history than three sessions, so it sits at the population default.

    How it decides

    Every set you log updates an estimated one-rep max for that exercise. Prescriptions come off the trend across recent sessions rather than a single good day, so one heavy session doesn't send the next one somewhere you can't follow. The newest session carries four sevenths of the answer, the one before it two, the one before that one — no single rating can ever be more than about half of it.

    It also learns how you rate effort. Reps-in-reserve judgement is a skill, and most lifters under-rate what they have left — which quietly drags every prescription too light. LiftMax measures your personal bias and corrects for it.

    Come back after time off and it backs the load off

    Not by waiting for you to prove the loss with a bad session — by the calendar, before you lift. Under ten days nothing happens, because strength is the slowest thing to decay and a holiday costs almost nothing. Past six weeks it stops scaling, because the loss is no longer predictable from time alone and your next few sessions are better evidence than any curve.

    Knows when a lift has run out of room.

    Mark the top of the rack or the last pin on the stack, and it stops prescribing past what the equipment holds — and says so, rather than letting the weight sit still for weeks looking like a plateau.

    LiftMax Pro

    The free app tells you what to lift today. Pro plans the weeks ahead and reads back what they did.

    One payment, yours for good — no subscription. That is possible because there is no server behind LiftMax: one more lifter costs nothing to run, so there is no monthly bill to pass on. Buy it once and it unlocks on every device signed in to the same Google Play or App Store account.

    Programs

    Run a multi-week block with every session scheduled ahead of time, deload weeks included, instead of picking a routine each day. Build one yourself, or answer a few questions — goal, days a week, session length, equipment, anything to work around — and have it drafted for you, then change any part of it.

    • Sessions are in order, not on dates — miss a week and the block waits for you rather than breaking.
    • A check-in while it runs, and a review at the end of what the block actually moved.
    • Finished blocks stay in Past programs, ready to run again.

    MaxStats

It opens on a verdict — how many lifts are going up, and anything that needs a look — then goes as deep as you want: hard sets per muscle each week against what is enough and what is too much, a muscle heat map, records, consistency, how close you came to your targets, and when you're on course to hit a goal.

    Over any date range, and on every exercise, routine, program and workout.

    Session insights

    While you lift: what today's fatigue is doing to each target, and which sets are carrying the session.

    On any workout, running or finished — and it fills in as you go.

    Routine review

    A routine read against your own training — which lifts are moving and which have stalled, what to change in the sets and reps, a swap for a lift that has stopped, and which parts of a muscle the routine never reaches.

    What stays free

    Logging, history, progression, backup and export stay free forever. So does everything the free app already does — the reps-in-reserve calibration, your personal rep-max curve, strength standards, warm-ups, the rest timer — and there is no limit on exercises, routines or workouts. Pro adds to the app; it never takes anything away from it.

    Your data stays yours

    No account. No sign-up. No ads.

    Everything you enter — your profile, workouts, routines and history — is stored in a database on your own device. There is no server holding your training history, because there is no server. Back up to your own Google Drive on Android or iCloud on iPhone, so a lost or replaced phone doesn't mean starting over — encrypted if you want, and only if you switch it on. Erase everything from within the app whenever you want.

    Pro doesn't change any of that. The purchase is held by Google or Apple against your store account; the app only asks whether that account owns it.

    Anonymous crash reports are sent to help fix bugs. They contain technical diagnostics only — never your training data. Details are in the privacy policy.
